# Defined.ai

> Defined.ai is the leading marketplace for ethical AI training data, helping AI teams build, scale, and deploy high-quality models with confidence. The platform offers ready-to-use datasets across speech, text, image, video, and multimodal, plus custom data collection, annotation, machine translation, conversational AI, LLM fine-tuning, and data and model evaluation services. Data is sourced from a global community of 1.6M+ vetted experts across 150+ markets and 500+ languages, dialects, and locales, with full provenance and compliance: ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, GDPR, and HIPAA.

Founded in 2015 by Daniela Braga, Defined.ai is headquartered in Seattle, with offices in Lisbon, Porto, and Tokyo. Customers include Fortune 500 enterprises, foundation model providers, and AI-first companies in technology, healthcare, finance, automotive, and other regulated industries.

## Why Defined.ai (key differentiators)

- **Ethical sourcing**: All data is sourced with informed consent and fair compensation.
- **Scale and diversity**: 1.6M+ contributors across 150+ markets and 500+ languages, including low-resource language pairs.
- **Compliance**: ISO 27001, ISO 27701, ISO 42001, GDPR, and HIPAA.
- **Marketplace + custom**: The only major provider offering both off-the-shelf datasets AND custom data services through the same crowd.
